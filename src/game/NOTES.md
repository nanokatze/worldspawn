## Weapon design

The general weapon design principle we should stick to is that different
loadouts should emphasize different play styles, rewarding some play style and
punishing the other. Loadouts should not be "DND counters" to other loadouts or
classes (e.g. Razorback in TF2.)

## Healing and ammo

We'll want to avoid adding medic into the game. Making players drop medkits and
ammo on death is interesting.

## Classes

A class is defined by its walk speed, starting health, hitboxes and animations,
and the conservative physics shape.

Sniper:

A sniper rifle should have an always-on laser so that everyone can note sniper's
sightline. The sniper rifle should do D damage, with great knockback. The
knockback helps the sniper contribute more, potentially getting environment
kills as well as keep enemies away. The player may choose to charge up their
sniper rifle by holding down the left mouse button, and T time, the sniper rifle
shoots with N*D damage, where a good value for N is probably around 2 or 3. This
should make camping less practical.
